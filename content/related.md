title: Related projects
description: The projects around Projectile
---

Projectile does a lot on its own, but over the years a bunch of packages have
grown around it - some integrate it with other parts of Emacs, some add extra
features for particular kinds of projects. Here are the most notable ones.

## Comes with Projectile

- **[projectile-consult](https://github.com/bbatsov/projectile/blob/master/projectile-consult.el)** -
  a [Consult](https://github.com/minad/consult) integration that streams
  candidates into the minibuffer as the project gets indexed.

## Integrations

- **[helm-projectile](https://github.com/bbatsov/helm-projectile)** - a
  [Helm](https://github.com/emacs-helm/helm) interface for Projectile.
- **[counsel-projectile](https://github.com/lafrenierejm/counsel-projectile)** -
  Ivy and Counsel integration.
- **[persp-projectile](https://github.com/bbatsov/persp-projectile)** - a
  separate [perspective](https://github.com/nex3/perspective-el) for every
  project.
- **[treemacs](https://github.com/Alexander-Miller/treemacs)** - the file tree,
  which ships a `treemacs-projectile` integration.

## Extra features

- **[projectile-rails](https://github.com/Silex/projectile-rails)** - extra
  navigation and commands for Ruby on Rails projects.
- **[org-project-capture](https://github.com/colonelpanic8/org-project-capture)**
  (formerly org-projectile) - Org TODOs tied to your projects.

## More

The manual keeps a [list of extensions](https://docs.projectile.mx/projectile/extensions.html),
and if you're after a ready-made Emacs setup with Projectile in it, there's
always [Prelude](https://github.com/bbatsov/prelude).
